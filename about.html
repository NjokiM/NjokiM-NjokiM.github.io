● About

Njoki Mwihaki

I'm a GeoAI practitioner and graduate geospatial engineer based in Nairobi, Kenya. I work at the intersection of geospatial technology and artificial intelligence — building systems that make sense of place, space, and the data that describes them.

I started this blog — Spatial Tidbits — because I learn by writing. Every article is my attempt to understand something deeply enough to explain it clearly. If it helps someone else along the way, even better.

What I write about

My writing spans geospatial engineering, machine learning, Python, SQL, and the occasional reflection on what it means to work in this field. I try to make technical concepts accessible without oversimplifying them.

Background

I'm a graduate geospatial engineer with hands-on experience in spatial data science, GIS, remote sensing, and applying machine learning to geographic problems. I believe the world makes more sense when you can put it on a map — and more sense still when you can interrogate that map intelligently.

Selected work
