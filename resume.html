Selected software engineering

BoardLot Africa — subscription & payments platform

Django 5 · PostgreSQL · Cloud9 / M-Pesa

A membership platform for a reader-supported publication: visitors choose a tier, pay by M-Pesa STK Push, and access is granted automatically. Built end to end — data model, payment integration, admin, responsive front end, test suite and deployment configuration.

  • Three independent locks on the payment callback. A per-record token in the URL path, a constant-time comparison of an HMAC header secret, and a provider re-fetch that must independently confirm success and amount before a record is marked paid. Each fails closed.
  • Idempotent by construction. A provider retry cannot double-charge or grant a second year; duplicate callbacks resolve to a no-op and the row lock serialises concurrent delivery.
  • Append-only financial ledger. Enforced at four levels — model save(), delete(), the queryset layer and a database constraint — so a correction is a new compensating entry, never an edit. Financial history cannot be rewritten, by anyone, including me.
  • Transactional integrity. Membership activation happens inside the same transaction as the payment confirmation, so a failure rolls money back rather than recording a payment against no access. Outbound email is deferred to on_commit: a broken mailer cannot un-pay a reader.
  • Verified, not asserted. 157 automated tests, plus production settings that refuse to boot without secrets, and layout verified by DevTools Protocol measurement rather than eyeballed screenshots.
